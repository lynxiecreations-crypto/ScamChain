ScamChain separates the mailbox you use from the identity a message claims. Select where you received it, then inspect the message itself. Gmail or Outlook is not automatically the sender's true identity.

WHAT THE CHECKS MEAN

Three authentication signals

  • SPF: whether the sending server is authorized for the envelope-sender domain.
  • DKIM: whether a domain signature is reported as valid.
  • DMARC: whether SPF or DKIM aligns with the visible From domain under the domain's policy.

These values are read from pasted headers; this browser demo does not perform cryptographic validation or live DNS lookups. Authentication is one signal, not a complete verdict: mailbox providers may also consider sender reputation, history and behavior.

Research references: Google sender guidelines · Microsoft 365 authentication · DMARC standard (RFC 7489)
